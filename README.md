# Welcome to my music 🎵🎬

A portable, responsive, offline-ready Music and Video Player built for **both Laptop and Mobile** with a comprehensive **Design Studio** and custom theme creator.

---

## ✨ Features & Highlights

### 💻 Portable for Laptop and Mobile
- **Laptop & Desktop Experience**:
  - Automatically activates a **Two-Column Workstation Dashboard** on widescreen displays.
  - **Left Column**: Library, All Songs, Video collection, Playlists, Search, and Profile settings.
  - **Right Column**: Sticky **16:9 Landscape Video & Audio Visualizer Stage**, Live Karaoke Lyrics marquee, Seeker, Transport controls, Volume slider, Speed selector, Equalizer, and Lyrics & Sync Studio.
- **Mobile Experience**:
  - Seamless 100% responsive fluid mobile layout.
  - Floating bottom mini player with real-time scrub progress.
  - Slide-up full player modal sheet with touch-friendly gestures.
  - Fixed mobile bottom navigation dock (**Home**, **Search**, **Add**, **Library**, **Design**, **Settings**).
- **Layout Switcher**: Choose between *Auto (two columns on laptop)*, *Two columns*, or *Single column* in the Design panel at any time.

---

### 🎨 Design Studio & "Add Your Own Creations"
Open **Design** from the top header or bottom menu bar to personalize:
- **Colour & Theme**:
  - Presets: *Auto (system)*, *Midnight*, *Ocean*, *Rose*, *Forest*, *Sunset*, *Violet*, *Amber*, *Pure Light*, *OLED Black*, *Cyberpunk*, *Retro Synth*.
  - **✨ Create Your Own Theme**: Pick your own Accent, Secondary gradient, Background base, Card surface, and Text colors using live color pickers.
- **Frames**:
  - *Rounded*, *Square*, *Circle*, *Neon* (glowing aura), *Polaroid* (classic photo frame), *Film* (35mm perforated reels), *Glass* (frosted glassmorphism), *Ticket* (perforated coupons), *Vinyl* (rotating 33 RPM phonograph disc), *Cyberpunk* (futuristic HUD angles), *Cassette* (vintage twin-reel tape).
  - Works on videos and live audio visualizers!
- **Bottom controls size**: *Small*, *Medium*, *Large*.
- **Bottom bar**: *Plain*, *Floating Dock*, *Glassmorphism*, *Neon Glow*, *Minimal*.
- **Text size**: *Small*, *Normal*, *Large*.
- **Subtitle size (Lyrics & Meta)**: *Small*, *Normal*, *Large*, *Extra Large*.
- **List spacing**: *Compact*, *Comfortable*, *Spacious*.
- **Background style**: *Gradient*, *Plain*, *Radial Glow*, *Mesh Aurora*, *Starry Night*, *Glass*.
- **Page layout**: *Auto (two columns on laptop)*, *Two columns*, *Single column*.
- *"Changes apply as you pick them. Save design closes this panel. Open Design from the menu bar to change them again."*

### 🎵 Audio & Video Controls
- **16:9 Landscape Stage**: Default 16:9 aspect ratio with quick cycle button for 4:3, 1:1, and 21:9 ultrawide.
- **Full**: Fullscreen / theater mode toggle.
- **Snap 📸**: Capture high-definition frame snapshots from any video with one-click image download.
- **Seeker & Timers**: High precision scrub slider with `0:00` / `0:00` duration timestamps.
- **Transport controls**:
  - `⏮` Previous track
  - `−10s` Rewind 10 seconds
  - `▶` / `⏸` Play / Pause (or press `Spacebar`)
  - `+10s` Fast forward 10 seconds
  - `⏭` Next track
  - `↻1` Repeat one track
  - `↻All` Repeat all tracks
  - `🔀` Shuffle mode
- **Volume**: Slider with exact percentage badge (e.g. `80%`) and boost amplification up to 150% / 200% with headphone hearing protection warning.
- **Speed**: `0.5x`, `0.75x`, `1x`, `1.25x`, `1.5x`, `2x`.
- **Audio Mastering & Safety**:
  - *Clean sound engine*: Low-pass & high-pass filtering to soften tape hiss and background rumble.
  - *Headphones Safe Mode*: Bass softening, treble smoothing, and volume limit to protect ears.
- **Live Canvas Audio Visualizer**: 3 selectable visualizer modes (*Gradient Bars*, *Smooth Neon Wave*, *Circular Radar Spectrum*).

---

### 🎤 Live Synced Lyrics & Karaoke Studio
- Real-time glowing karaoke lyrics marquee.
- Interactive lyric lines: click any line to instantly jump playback to that second!
- **Auto sync**: Automatically spreads lyric lines evenly across the song.
- **Manual sync**: Tap the **⏱ Stamp line** button when each lyric line starts.
- Lyrics editor: Paste or update lyrics anytime.

---

### 📁 Library & Playlist Management
- **Add to my music**:
  - Add Song (Audio) or Add Video.
  - Drag and drop or browse files.
  - Custom Title, Artist, Playlist selection, and Lyrics.
  - Automatic video thumbnail generation using HTML5 canvas.
- **Options Menu (⋯)**:
  - *Play Now*
  - *Play next* (inserts immediately after current song in queue)
  - *Add to playlist*
  - *Remove from playlist*
  - *Delete track*
- **Playlists**: Create custom playlists, filter songs by playlist, or delete playlists.
- **Built-in Demo Generator**: Two pre-generated synthwave and lofi beat tracks included so you can start listening immediately without needing to upload files first.
- **Backup & Restore**: Export your full library and design settings as a JSON file, and restore on any device.

---

## 🚀 How to Run

1. Simply double-click **`launch.bat`** or open **`index.html`** in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Firefox, or Safari.
2. No internet connection or installation required. All data is saved locally on your device in IndexedDB and localStorage.
